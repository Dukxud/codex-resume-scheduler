Codex Resume Scheduler — LIVE 3D MATERIAL BAR PREVIEW

This is an experimental, fully functioning Windows preview based on v1.11.0.
The four comparison bars are live Windows Presentation Foundation (WPF)
ProgressBar controls hosted inside the real scheduler, not pictures.

Run:
1. Extract the ENTIRE versioned ZIP into a new folder.
2. Double-click Start-Codex-Scheduler.vbs (PowerShell 7 is required).
3. In standard/full mode, click "Compare 3D bars" next to "Usage remaining".
4. Move the percentage slider. All four bars respond independently to the
   sample percentage, and their highlights animate while the value is fixed.

This preview does NOT replace the existing usage or countdown bars, does NOT
save a preferred style, and does NOT modify scheduled jobs or preferences.

For visual evidence, the Windows CI build captures actual WPF control pixels
at 68% and a sequence of real animation frames. They are not generated art.

The production release remains v1.11.0. The display quality of this
experimental preview has NOT been approved.
